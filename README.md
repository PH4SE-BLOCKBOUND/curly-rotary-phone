# BLOCKBOUND
### Build Your World. Discover the Unknown.

BLOCKBOUND is an original 3D voxel sandbox adventure where you can explore endless worlds, build amazing structures, discover mysterious caves, meet unique creatures, and uncover rare secrets.

Explore, create, and shape your own adventure. Your world, your rules!

## Features
- Massive, expanding worlds
- Mountains, forests, rivers, and lakes
- Mysterious caves and underground exploration
- Unique original creatures
- Rare Nightmare Variants
- Creative building and exploration
- Detailed pixel-art graphics
- Immersive environments and lighting

## Development Status
BLOCKBOUND is currently in development. More features, creatures, and world improvements are planned.

**Your world. Your adventure. Your BLOCKBOUND.**
